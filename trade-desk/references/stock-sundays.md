# Stock Sundays / Aristotle Investments — Swing Trade Framework

Slow-money consistency over speculative swings. Reactive, not predictive: wait for price to confirm, don't guess tops/bottoms.

## Top-down read (do this in order, don't skip a level)

1. **Weekly chart** — overall trend/regime. Is the market (and the name) in an uptrend, downtrend, or chop? This sets the bias for everything below.
2. **Daily chart** — structure within that trend: recent highs/lows, key levels, where a neckline or range boundary sits.
3. **5-minute chart** — the actual trigger. This is where the neckline break and the 15-Cent Rule get applied.

Always reference the broader tape too — SPY, QQQ, IWM — even if the trade itself is a single name. A break on a stock means less if the relevant index is fighting the opposite direction.

## Entry trigger: neckline break

- Identify the neckline (the level price needs to reclaim/break to confirm the setup — from a range, a failed breakdown, a base, etc.) on the daily chart.
- Wait for price to actually break it on the 5-min chart. Don't anticipate the break — react to it.

## The 15-Cent Rule

- Once the neckline breaks, require an additional **$0.15 of follow-through beyond the level** before entering, to filter out fakeouts/wicks that barely poke through and reverse.
- If price breaks the neckline but can't clear it by 15 cents, no trade — stand down and wait for the next setup.

## Position sizing: thirds

- Every position gets built in **three tranches**, not all at once.
- First third on initial confirmed entry (neckline + 15¢).
- Remaining thirds added on further confirmation (e.g., a pullback that holds, or continuation strength) — never averaging into a trade that's moving against the thesis.
- This caps risk on any single entry and lets the trade prove itself before full size is committed.

## Fixed risk discipline

- Risk is defined and fixed *before* entry, not adjusted after the fact.
- If the setup invalidates (price fails back below the neckline after entry, etc.), the stop is the stop — no moving it to "give it room."
